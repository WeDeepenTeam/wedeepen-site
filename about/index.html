About WeDeepen

Good at everything. Except this.

You built a career, a life, a body you take care of. Love is the one thing no one trained you for. WeDeepen is where accomplished people finally learn it, with world-class faculty and a community doing the same.

Founder · CEO

Christina Weber

Christina Weber didn’t begin her career in the relationship industry. She spent a decade in corporate America, from pharmaceutical sales to helping launch and grow what became Seamless and Grubhub, where she became a top salesperson.

She also brought her talent for gathering people around meaningful causes to philanthropy, chairing major events in New York City and later founding Feminine Weapon, which has raised more than $100,000 for children experiencing abuse, poverty, and trafficking.

Then her attention turned toward something more personal:

What does it take to be good at love?

In 2014, Christina created Underground Unattached in New York City. What she learned sent her into more than a decade of studying personal development, relationships, intimacy, and human connection. She became an ICF-certified coach, trained at iPEC, and immersed herself in the work of leading teachers and methodologies.

But Christina’s particular talent was finding exceptional people and ideas, bringing them together, and turning what she discovered into experiences people could actually participate in.

That instinct became WeDeepen.

Since then, Christina has created and curated relationship experiences that have reached more than 40,000 people. Her work has landed on the cover of LA Magazine, appeared on Peacock’s Ex-Rated and Real Time with Bill Maher, and brought her to stages and institutions including Omega Institute and leading personal-development and biohacking conferences.

She is also the host of Mastering Love with Christina Weber, with more than 180 episodes exploring love, intimacy, dating, and relationships with leading voices in the field.

Today, Christina is building WeDeepen around the belief that love is a trainable skill, and that getting good at it may be some of the most important work we do.

Managing Partner

René Besson

For more than thirty years, René Besson produced film and television across independent and studio environments, with credits including The Expendables, The Mechanic, Stone, Righteous Kill, Trespass, Ordinary Love, and Driven, working with talent including Robert De Niro, Al Pacino, Nicole Kidman, Liam Neeson, Sylvester Stallone, Jason Statham, and Michael Caine.

Producing taught René how to see the whole system: people, resources, timing, emotion, and execution, and get them moving in the same direction.

He first came to WeDeepen as an advisor, helping Christina think about how to tell its story. René often returned to Roger Ebert’s description of movies as “a machine that generates empathy.” One day, Christina offered a reframe: Replace “movies” with “WeDeepen.”

It clicked.

Film lets us experience another person’s perspective through observation. René saw that WeDeepen could create empathy through participation, giving people a place to meet, be vulnerable, practice something new, and build relationships that continue beyond the experience.

He came in to help tell the story of WeDeepen. He found himself becoming part of his next big one.

Today, René works alongside Christina to turn the vision into an executable system, leading strategy, operations, business development, partnerships, storytelling, and fundraising support.

The Story

How WeDeepen Began

It started in 2014, in New York City. Christina was hosting Underground Unattached, gathering smart, accomplished single people in one room, believing that proximity plus quality would lead to love.

For two years she watched. The right people were in the room. But the room wasn’t enough.

Pressure. Expectations. The nervous energy people walked in with. Christina began to realize that finding the right person was only part of the equation. People also needed to learn how to create and sustain great relationships.

Then couples started showing up asking: What about us?

Christina went looking for answers and discovered an entire industry dedicated to love as a learnable skill: relationship educators, coaches, therapists, researchers, somatic practitioners, intimacy teachers, and frameworks most people never knew existed.

She went deep. Got trained. Studied with the best. And eventually asked the question that would become WeDeepen:

Why isn’t there one trusted place that brings the best of this together?

So she built it.

Today, WeDeepen is a curated ecosystem for relationship education and human connection. More than 40,000 people have participated in the experiences Christina began creating over a decade ago.

The premise remains simple:

Love is a skill. You can study it. Practice it. And become extraordinary at it.

Our Values

WeDeepen with GUIDANCE

Eight values that shape how we show up: with each other, with our members, and with ourselves.

Growth comes first

You believe in feeling a sense of purpose, and that purpose is growth. Individual growth. Collective growth. The kind of growth that makes the world a better place.

Unoffendable

Anger is extraordinarily easy. It's our default setting. Love is challenging. Love is a miracle. You grow in love.

Intuitive flow

When operating from the soul level, you flow.

Desire to receive for the sake of sharing

There is no enjoying the possession of anything valuable unless you have ones to share it with. You give to enjoy the shared life.

Accepts change

Progress is impossible without change, and those who cannot change their minds cannot change anything. You can change.

Nurture, self-discovery & freewill

Knowing yourself is the beginning of all wisdom. You trust your life journey and the lessons it's teaching you. No one will care for you better than you.

Curiosity & thoughtful discourse

It is not our differences that divide us. It is our inability to recognize, accept and celebrate those differences. You lead with curiosity.

Essentially, our wholeness depends on relationships

The quality of my life is determined by the quality of my relationships. You love thy neighbor.
