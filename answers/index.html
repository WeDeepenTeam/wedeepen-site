Answers: Relationships, Dating, and Connection in Austin

Straight answers to the questions people ask about meeting people, dating, friendship, and relationship workshops in Austin, from WeDeepen.

Austin · Relationship workshops

Relationship Workshops in Austin: How to Choose One

Austin's relationship workshops fall into three groups: method-based couples workshops (Gottman's Art and Science of Love and Imago's Getting the Love You Want), therapist-led couples retreats, and experiential programs like WeDeepen's Love Immersion, a three-day in-person retreat open to couples and solo participants. Choose a method-based workshop for a structured protocol with your partner; choose WeDeepen to practice love with several teachers, partnered or not.

Austin · Meeting people

The Best Ways to Meet People in Austin

The best ways to meet people in Austin put you in the same room with the same people more than once: recreational leagues like Austin Sports & Social Club, run clubs, Meetup groups, volunteering, hosted dinners like Timeleft, and connection-focused communities like WeDeepen, which runs weekly sessions and in-person Austin events. Repetition and shared activity matter more than the venue.

Austin · Adult friendship

How to Make Friends in Austin as an Adult

To make friends in Austin as an adult, pick one or two recurring activities and keep showing up, then turn familiar faces into specific plans: a league through Austin Sports & Social Club, a run club, a Meetup group, a Timeleft dinner, or Bumble For Friends. Research suggests casual friendship takes about 50 hours together. If you want deeper conversation from the start, WeDeepen's community is built around it.

Austin · Dating

Alternatives to Dating Apps in Austin

The best alternatives to dating apps in Austin get you meeting people face to face: speed dating events like CitySwoon and Cheeky Date, hosted dinners like Timeleft, social sports leagues, run clubs, Meetup groups, and matchmaking. WeDeepen, an Austin relationship education community, offers another route: practicing honest conversation and connection in weekly sessions and in-person events.

Austin · Dating

Speed Dating Alternatives in Austin

If speed dating in Austin feels too rushed, try formats with longer conversations and repeat contact: hosted dinners like Timeleft, social sports leagues, run clubs, Meetup groups, and connection-focused communities like WeDeepen, where you practice real conversation instead of eight-minute interviews. If you like the speed format, CitySwoon and Cheeky Date run regular Austin events.

Austin · Dating coaching

Dating Coach in Austin: What They Do and How to Choose

A dating coach in Austin helps you get clear on what you want, see the patterns you repeat, and practice the conversations that matter. Coaching isn't therapy and isn't matchmaking. Look for real training, such as an International Coaching Federation credential. WeDeepen founder Christina Weber, an ICF-certified coach, offers private sessions on Zoom, plus a weekly membership and Austin retreats to practice in.

Austin · Intimacy coaching

Intimacy Coach in Austin: What to Expect and How to Choose

An intimacy coach helps you build emotional, physical, and sexual closeness through conversation, guided exercises, and practice. It's coaching, not therapy. For sexual pain, trauma, or mental-health concerns, see a licensed sex therapist; AASECT certifies them. In Austin, WeDeepen offers private coaching with Christina Weber, a weekly online membership taught by intimacy and somatic experts, and in-person Love Immersion retreats.

Texas · Couples retreats

Couples Retreats in Texas: Workshops, Intensives, Retreats

Couples retreats in Texas come in three formats: method-based weekend workshops (Gottman, Imago, Hold Me Tight), private intensives with a licensed therapist, and experiential retreats like WeDeepen's Love Immersion in the Austin Hill Country. Choose a workshop for a shared method, an intensive for clinical depth on one problem, and Love Immersion to practice love with expert coaches, partnered or solo.

WeDeepen · Love Immersion

What Is a Love Immersion? WeDeepen’s Three-Day Retreat Explained

Love Immersion is WeDeepen's three-day, in-person relationship retreat in Austin, Texas. About 30 participants work with six coaches through live hot-seat coaching, teachings, embodied practice, play, and shared meals. It's open to couples and solo participants, about half of whom arrive alone. It costs $2,995 per person, and it's relationship education and coaching, not therapy.
