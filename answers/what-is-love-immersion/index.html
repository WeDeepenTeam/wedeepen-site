What Is a Love Immersion? WeDeepen’s Three-Day Retreat Explained

By WeDeepen · October 4, 2026

The short answer

Love Immersion is WeDeepen's three-day, in-person relationship retreat in Austin, Texas. About 30 participants work with six coaches through live hot-seat coaching, teachings, embodied practice, play, and shared meals. It's open to couples and solo participants, about half of whom arrive alone. It costs $2,995 per person, and it's relationship education and coaching, not therapy.

A Love Immersion is WeDeepen's three-day, in-person relationship retreat in Austin, Texas. The format is a "love mastermind": about 30 participants and six coaches, built around live hot-seat coaching, plus teachings, embodied practice, play, and shared meals. You can come solo or with a partner. This page covers what happens over the three days, who goes, what it costs, and how it differs from a workshop or therapy.

Where the name comes from

Most of us reach adulthood with years of education and almost none in love. We follow chemistry, learn through heartbreak, and hope experience eventually turns into wisdom. Christina Weber, who founded WeDeepen, designed Love Immersion to make that learning intentional. The idea is total immersion, the way you'd learn a language: three days in which love is the only subject. The tagline for the current edition is "Mastermind your love life."

What happens over three days

The center of the weekend is the Masterminding Love hot seat. You bring what's most alive in your love life, whether that's a decision, a pattern, a conversation you're dreading, or a desire you haven't named, to two expert coaches. They help you see what's really happening and how to move forward. Then you learn again by watching everyone else take their turn. Other people's questions, blind spots, and courage often show you things you couldn't see in your own life.

Around the hot seats, the weekend includes:

  • Workshops and interactive exercises for clearer, more connected relationships.
  • Play and connection: Truth or Dare, games, and surprises.
  • Shared meals, all included.
  • Integration and a graduation ceremony on the last day, with community support after.

The October 2026 edition, Love Immersion VIII, runs Saturday through Monday. The first day starts at 9 AM with breakfast and an opening circle, and the retreat closes around 2 PM on the third day. The full schedule is on the Love Immersion VIII page.

Who teaches it

There's no single guru or method. The faculty are senior coaches Christina chose because they're the people she calls for guidance in her own love life. Love Immersion VIII is led by Christina Weber with Annie Lalla, Ariana Hall, Yossef Sagi, Jordan Bowditch, Rachel Cuthbert, Yarixa Ferrao, and Scott Wild. Each brings a different lens, from relationship dynamics and polarity to nervous-system healing and the one hard conversation a relationship needs. Full bios are on the faculty page.

Who goes

  • People who are dating, partnered, between relationships, or deciding whether to stay.
  • About half arrive solo. Others come with a partner, a friend, a sibling, or a parent.
  • Ages have ranged from 18 to 87, mostly people in their thirties through sixties.
  • Relationship coaches often attend as participants.

You don't need a partner, a breakup, or a crisis to come. Participants rate it 9.6 out of 10, and 70% come back. One participant, Eva Mullen, said it this way: "I didn't think I belonged. But within a day, I felt flooded with love. I felt home." More accounts are on the reviews page.

The rules of the room

Every Love Immersion runs on WeDeepen's Four Pillars: growth comes first, with no attachment to outcome; you own your own experience; all feelings are welcome, and no inner process is condemned; and the relationship is a place to practice love. In practice, that means you choose how far to go in any exercise, and nothing you feel is off-limits to bring.

Cost, venue, and logistics

  • Price: $2,995 per person, with a payment plan at checkout.
  • Refunds: tickets are non-refundable but fully transferable.
  • Venue: HAVAN Estates, a private space in the Texas Hill Country at 17301 Flint Rock Rd, Austin, TX 78738.
  • Meals: included. Lodging is booked separately, with some onsite options.
  • Travel: fly into Austin-Bergstrom (AUS).

Love Club members, at $9,997/year, get one Love Immersion credit, transferable to a partner.

Between retreats

Three days can shift a lot, and love is a trainable skill, so the practice continues. The WeDeepen Membership, $99/month or $990/year, gives you a live session every Tuesday at 6 PM Central with the same faculty, and a private community. Join the membership. Come play with us.

FAQ

Is Love Immersion therapy?

No. It's relationship education and coaching, not therapy or a substitute for mental-health treatment.

Do I need to bring a partner?

No. About half of participants come solo.

How many people attend?

About 30 participants, with six coaches, for Love Immersion VIII.

When is the next Love Immersion?

Love Immersion VIII is October 17 to 19, 2026, in Austin. Future dates are listed on the events page.

How is it different from a Gottman or Imago workshop?

Those teach one research-based method to couples. Love Immersion uses several coaches and live hot-seat work, and welcomes solo participants.
