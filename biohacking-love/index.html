Why this weekend

Nobody trained you for big love.

We’re changing that.

You’ve optimized your sleep, your supplements, your training, your morning routine. The most consequential operating system in your life is still running on factory settings — because no one ever taught you how to upgrade it.

Biohacking Love is a two-day intensive designed for individuals and couples ready to bring the same rigor to relating that they bring to the body and mind. Storytelling, frameworks, embodied practice, and live coaching — held by a small council of facilitators who actually live the work.

Two days. One room. A different operating system by the time you leave.

The Foundation

Four Pillars of a Conscious Relationship

The frame the day is built on. Simple enough to remember. Deep enough to practice for a lifetime.

01

Growth comes first

No attachment to outcome. The point isn’t to be right, win, or get what you wanted — it’s to grow.

02

Each person owns their own sh*t

Your reactions are your inheritance to tend. Theirs are theirs. Sovereignty makes intimacy possible.

03

All feelings are welcome

No internal process is condemned. Anger, grief, jealousy, fear, longing — nothing in you needs to be hidden to belong.

04

The relationship is the practice

Love isn’t a destination you arrive at. It’s a place to practice — daily, awkwardly, beautifully — for the rest of your life.

The Council

Christina & the council

Each guest brings a distinct lineage — somatic, therapeutic, ceremonial, polaric. Together they hold a room that’s rare on its own and almost impossible to find in one day.

Your facilitator

Christina Weber

Founder of WeDeepen and host of the Deepen with Christina podcast, Christina has spent over a decade building the connective tissue between the world’s most playful, integrous love practitioners and the people ready to learn alongside them.

Past Deepen guests include John Gray, the Hendricks, Dave Asprey, Ken Page, LCSW, and dozens of practitioners shaping the field. Today, she’s leading the room.

Breath, Sound, Frequency

Pavel Aeön

Co-founder of Upper Aeön and creator of the BDM Method™ — Breathe, Dance, Meditate. Former international DJ turned consciousness guide, certified Wim Hof instructor, and impact entrepreneur. Pavel has led transformational work for 100,000+ people across 100+ immersive events a year.

upperaeon.com

Truth & Tenderness

Ariana Hall

Ariana works in the precise territory between truth and tenderness. Her coaching cuts through the stories people have been telling themselves for years — and holds them steady while they feel what was underneath. Known for clarity without cruelty: the strategist you go to when you’re ready to stop dressing up the same pattern in new clothes.

arianahall.com

Relational Philosopher & Erotic Blueprints Expert

Kayce Neill

Kayce is a relational philosopher and advisor on love, desire, power, and aliveness for people who have already done the work and are ready to live it. Her work explores intimacy, human dynamics, and the space between knowing yourself and becoming yourself, helping people move beyond performance and into deeper connections, richer desires, and lives that feel fully expressed.

kayceneill.com

Radical Play & Polarity

Jordan Bowditch

Jordan teaches the embodied mechanics of polarity — the magnetic tension between masculine and feminine energy that keeps long-term relationships alive. His work invites radical play, presence, and the kind of permission most people have never been given to desire fully. Expect somatic work, direct coaching, and a rare fusion of warmth and precision.

@jordanbowditch
