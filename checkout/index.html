Love is a trainable skill

Get even better at love. Have fun doing it.

Every great love story has a messy middle

We build skills in work, health, and sport through learning, practice, and feedback. Love is usually left to trial and error. WeDeepen gives you a place to build it on purpose, including learning to tell the difference between:

  • Chemistry versus alignment
  • A rough patch versus a repeating pattern
  • Healthy compromise versus losing yourself
  • A real concern versus an old fear getting triggered
  • A boundary versus a wall

What you practice

  1. Choose relationships that align with the life you want.
  2. Feel desired, and keep desire alive over time.
  3. Turn conflict into closeness.
  4. Know what you want, and ask for it.
  5. Spot your patterns and choose new ones.
  6. Bring more play and pleasure into love.
  7. Have the hard conversations that bring you closer.
  8. Grow trust that can hold change and uncertainty.
