Show Notes

Last call! Love Immersion VIII is October 17 to 19 in Austin. The signature mastermind for your love life: hot seats, personalized coaching, and a community that keeps coming back. Scott Wild is a featured coach. Tickets: wedeepen.com

In this episode of Mastering Love with Christina Weber, Christina sits down with turning point conversation specialist Scott Wild to map the two capacities every secure relationship requires: sovereignty and devotion. Scott coaches people through the conversations they keep ruminating on, from feedback for a partner to the breakup talk they can’t find words for. He shares his path from anxious texting to secure attachment, the question that changed everything four days before he met his partner Mel, and the matching-outfits ritual that’s part devotion, part codependency test.

Main Topics Covered

• Turning Point Conversations: Moving from ruminating in loops to delivering the conversation cleanly, in a way you respect how you showed up. • Boundaries Are Decisions, Not Sentences: The real boundary is what you decide before you speak, and it only exists if you know what you’ll do when it isn’t met. • Secure Attachment, Defined: Scott’s north star: wanting something and being okay without it. Plus why attachment labels as identity hold you back. • From Maintenance to Creation: The question that switched Scott four days before meeting Mel: how can I add the most value to this relationship? • Criticism Is Half the Picture: Why the gap only lands when what’s going well is acknowledged first. • The Matching Ritual: Why Scott dresses to match Mel every day, and the backup plan that keeps devotion from becoming codependency. • Christina’s Burning Man Moment: The standoff that dissolved the second she remembered she could have fun on her own. • Devotion Without Scorekeeping: Doing it because that’s the kind of partner you are, not for the other person. • From Poly to All In: Scott’s honest reflection on his polyamorous years, and commitment as the removal of alternatives. • Building the Foundation: Sustainable pacing, never doing anything for a relationship you don’t want to do, and quarterly couple’s retreats.

Key Takeaways

• Sovereignty Plus Devotion: A secure relationship requires both: choosing toward yourself and choosing toward your partner. Most of us are only trained in one. • Look for Aligned, Not Right: Searching for “the one” puts pressure on a position to fill. Asking “is this an aligned partner” opens everything up. • Never Start with the Gap: Acknowledge what’s working first, or feedback lands as criticism. • Break the Codependent Opportunity: If you can’t do the thing without your partner, that’s the signal. Have the version you’d do alone. • Commitment Is the Removal of Alternatives: Within a month of choosing Mel, Scott’s income jumped six figures.

Connect with the Guest

About Scott Wild: Scott Wild is a turning point conversation specialist who helps people move from ruminating on a hard conversation to having it cleanly. A former software engineer, tech manager, and professional racquetball player once ranked 15th in the country, Scott coaches individuals and couples on boundaries, feedback, and the conversations that change lives. He lives in Austin with his partner Mel and their two young children, and is a featured coach at Love Immersion VIII.
