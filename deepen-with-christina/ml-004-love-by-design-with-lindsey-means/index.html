Show Notes

Big news! You can now join WeDeepen for just $99 a month: group sessions, love masterminds, and the top love and relationship teachers, all in one place. Come study love with us: wedeepen.com

In this episode of Mastering Love with Christina Weber, Christina sits down with Human Design expert and Identity Doula Lindsey Means to decode the modality Christina keeps returning to after more than a decade. Lindsey’s framing: your body is a self-driving Ferrari, and you’re designed to be the passenger princess. Human Design is the operation manual that gets you out of your head and back into trust. Together they walk through all five types, how each one best navigates dating, and how to love each type well, plus connection charts, conditioning, and why nobody should sleep in the same bed.

Main Topics Covered

- The Self-Driving Ferrari: Why conditioning has you grabbing the wheel of an all-intelligent vehicle, and how Human Design hands you the manual.

- Lindsey’s Story: From hairstylist to 1,200+ charts read, and the discovery that gave her permission to be herself: there’s nothing wrong with you.

- The Five Types: Manifestor, Generator, Manifesting Generator, Projector, and Reflector, and the movie analogy that makes them click.

- Dating by Type: Manifestors initiate and inform. Generators follow the ooh and respond. Projectors get recognized and read energetic invitations. Reflectors honor their 28-day lunar cycle.

- Loving Each Type: The signature frequencies to feed (peace, satisfaction, success, surprise and delight) and the not-self themes to watch for (anger, frustration, bitterness, disappointment).

- Deleting “I’m Single”: Why Christina says individuals, not singles, and Lindsey now says currently single or open for love.

- The Third Entity: Connection charts, sharing energy within six feet, and where couples escape the relationship.

- Where Conditioning Begins: Open centers, amplifying your family’s emotions, and why most relationship patterns are learned by age 12.

- Esther Perel’s Three Secrets: Missing and reuniting, seeing your partner through new eyes, and creating success together.

- Separate Bedrooms: The sleep chart rabbit hole, why a Projector never sleeps well next to a Generator, and normalizing your own bed.

- Christina’s News: She’s dating her ex-boyfriend, and the splenic Projector plus sacral Generator dance that comes with it.

Key Takeaways

- Strategy and Authority Are Enough: Everything else is beneficial, but these two are all you truly need to live your design.

- There’s Nothing Wrong With You: You’re not broken. You just haven’t discovered who you are and how to live it.

- Don’t Put Yourself in a Box: Type is the tip of the iceberg. Human Design is an experiment to live, not an excuse to hide behind.

- You Don’t Know Yourself Until You’ve Lived Alone: You are your conditioning until you realize it, and then you get to decondition.

- Love Is the Key, Fun Is the Way: None of this is that serious. Play with it.
