Show Notes

Blast off into 2027 with us! WeDeepen is throwing a love-filled, 100% sober New Year’s Eve party in Austin, Texas, limited to just 30 people. Reality Transurfing, manifestation, and the principles to realize your dreams. Plus, join the WeDeepen community with a 14-day free trial, then just $99 a month: wedeepen.com

In this episode of Mastering Love with Christina Weber, Christina sits down with Owen Hunt, aka Bootsy Greenwood, the United States expert on Reality Transurfing and new faculty member inside WeDeepen. Bootsy found the 900-page Russian framework in 2016 while taking a class on how to make friends, and it took him from depression and addiction to stand-up comedy stages, Netflix credits, and touring the world with KISS and the Impractical Jokers. Together they break down how to slide to the timeline of love you actually want, why guilt spells GOD, and what your heart has been trying to tell you underneath all that mental noise.

Main Topics Covered

- A Framework for Reality: Why Transurfing isn’t a self-help book or law of attraction. It’s the rules of the game, and learning them raises your odds.

- Infinity and the Alternatives: Whatever you want already exists in the space of variations. Jumping timelines means choosing a version of yourself, and every real choice cuts the other options away.

- Your Star Sector: The most authentic expression of your essence, where your genius shows up and your goal fits your soul.

- GOD Is a Pendulum: A Love Immersion participant’s discovery that guilt, obligation, and duty form an acronym, and why any of the three means something external is pulling on you.

- The Flower in the Vase: Conditional love picks the flower and gets three days. Unconditional love admires it where it grows, and that love is irresistible.

- Pendulums Explained: The energetic group-think structures behind addictions, identities, football stadiums, and even churches, and how to negotiate the deal instead of being drained by it.

- Heart and Mind: The little girl in the toy store, the rustle of the morning stars, and why the mind is an amazing servant but a terrible master.

- The Wheel of Fortune: Success is the secret to success. Celebrate the action instead of waiting on the outcome, and the momentum compounds.

- Do I Stay or Do I Go: Sometimes you have to break up to get the love you want, and how Christina’s own ended relationship upleveled into something freer.

- Truth or Dare: Bootsy paints his ideal relationship in 60 seconds, and Christina names the one quality that makes a relationship extraordinary: attunement.

Key Takeaways

- Choice Is the Whole Game: Real choice, not wishing. You can’t be every version of yourself, so pick the one that fits your essence.

- You Are Not Broken: What feels like an unchangeable identity is often a pendulum you got lured into, and you can renegotiate the relationship.

- Let the Heart Captain the Ship: When heart leads and mind handles logistics, the heart sings and the mind rubs its hands with glee.

- Give Yourself Credit for the Action: A page a day is a book in 270 days. Internal wins build the wave of fortune before any external result shows up.

- If You Don’t Know What to Do, Slow Down: Move slowly, create space, and the answer comes to you.
