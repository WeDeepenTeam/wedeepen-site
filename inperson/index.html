Why This Weekend

What would it be like if your love life was even better?

You’ve optimized your sleep, your bloodwork, your nervous system. And yet love is still the place that humbles you.

If you’re partnered: Maybe you’re coming home lethargic, quick-tempered, more drained by the person you love than by anyone else. Maybe you’re having the same fight you were having two years ago. Maybe the magic is missing and you’re quietly asking whether to stay or go. Or maybe you’re in a brand new relationship and want to do it right this time.

If you’re solo: Maybe you’re tired of dating apps and events that go nowhere. Maybe you can’t find someone as evolved as you are. Maybe you’re wondering if the person you’re looking for would even choose you.

Nobody trained you in love. Not school, not your parents, not the hundred books you’ve read. You’ve done the healing. You’re doing the work. Love is still the one skill no one taught you to practice deliberately.

That’s what this weekend is for.

Count Me In

The Weekend

Two Days, Fully Alive

Day One · Saturday · 10 AM – 5 PM

Play, Structure & Conscious Connection

The morning starts with nervous-system grounding, because skillful love is built from state, not just insight.

Christina then teaches her most trusted relationship frames, including the 4 Pillars: practical tools that reorient how you see dating, intimacy, and partnership, and give you language for patterns that have felt confusing or repetitive.

You’ll also zoom out on the Love Industry: what exists, what’s missing, and why love deserves the same intentional strategy you give your health and performance.

Guest sessions:

  • Melissa D. of BodyJoy Intimacy School on navigating erotic community spaces with confidence, including how a third can support a couple in keeping the spark alive.
  • Rachel Cuthbert on psychedelics and intimacy: which medicines support heart-opening, how to find a qualified facilitator, and what makes a safe, well-held experience.

After a midday lunch break, the afternoon opens into the Playful Portal, an embodied session where joy, curiosity, and healthy risk are practiced as skills. You’ll close the day with live storytelling from people who’ve transformed their love lives by treating love as a skill worth mastering, plus integration time so the work lands in your body, not just your notes.

Day Two · Sunday · 10 AM – 4 PM

Neverland, Clarity & Hot Seat Coaching

“Love has come to rule and transform; stay awake, my heart, stay awake.” — Rumi

The morning integrates what shifted overnight, then moves into Neverland, a guided session for loosening old identities and reopening possibility in love. Through reflection and mind-mapping, you’ll map your patterns, desires, and leverage points into usable clarity.

Then the event highlight: Hot Seat Coaching with master coaches Annie Lalla and Jordan Bowditch, where outdated relationship patterns get interrupted live, in real time.

You’ll close with final frameworks and a closing circle, and leave with tools you’ll keep using long after the weekend.
