What would it be like if your love life was even better?

You’re optimizing your sleep, your bloodwork, your nervous system. And yet love is still the place that humbles you.

If you’re partnered: Maybe you’re more drained by the person you love than by anyone else, having the same fight you had two years ago, or quietly asking whether to stay or go.

Or if you’re solo: Maybe you’re tired of dating apps and events that go nowhere. Maybe you can’t find someone as evolved as you are. Maybe you’re wondering if the person you’re looking for would even choose you, or even exists. Maybe you think you’re too old, or better off alone.

Maybe it’s all true. Or maybe the current paradigm isn’t working. Relationships are going through a major redesign. People are unsatisfied in love; people don’t know how to make relationships work.

And, believe it or not, this isn’t a bad thing. Because when systems break down, that’s when they change. It’s how we move toward conscious love.

That’s the whole premise of this weekend, and of the invitation you’re receiving.

So what exactly is a conscious loving relationship?

It’s a relationship that has a purpose, and that purpose is growth.

Individual growth. Growth as a couple. Growth that makes the world a better place.

As of now, most people get into relationships to satisfy their own personal needs. This might work for a few years, but eventually the relationship fails us, and we end up unsatisfied as a result.

But when you come together with the intention of growth, the relationship strives toward something much greater than gratification. The partnership becomes a journey of evolution, and the individuals have an opportunity to expand more than they could alone. Deep satisfaction and long-term fulfillment arise as a result.

So if you’re someone who feels called to take love to the next level, WeDeepen was made for you.

Sign Me Up

WeDeepen In-Person

Spend two days getting even better at love.

Here’s what we’ll do. Over two days, you’ll learn and practice the 4 Pillars of a Conscious Relationship:

  1. No Attachment to Outcome. Growth comes first.
  2. Radical Ownership. Each person owns their own sh*t.
  3. All Feelings Are Welcome. No internal process is condemned.
  4. Love Is a Practice. The relationship is a place to practice love.

With gratitude to Shelly Bullard for bringing the 4 Pillars of a Conscious Relationship to life for us to practice and pursue.

Day One · Saturday · 10 AM – 5 PM

“Love is always bestowed as a gift – freely, willingly and without expectation. We don’t love to be loved; we love to love.” — Leo Buscaglia

Morning: Pillar 1, No Attachment to Outcome

We start with nervous-system grounding, because skillful love is built from state, not just insight.

Then Christina teaches the first pillar: being more committed to growth than to making any relationship “work.” You’ll see why relationships built to preserve an outcome go stale, and what shifts when growth becomes the point, whether you’re dating, partnered, or starting over. She’ll share her most trusted relationship frames and give you language for patterns that have felt confusing or repetitive.

You’ll also zoom out on the Love Industry: what exists, what’s missing, and why love deserves the same intentional strategy you give your health and performance.

Afternoon: Pillar 3, All Feelings Are Welcome

Guest sessions:

  • Melissa D. of BodyJoy Intimacy School on navigating erotic community spaces with confidence, including how a third can support a couple in keeping the spark alive.
  • Rachel Cuthbert on psychedelics and intimacy: which medicines support heart-opening, how to find a qualified facilitator, and what makes a safe, well-held experience.

Then an embodiment session where joy, curiosity, and healthy risk are practiced as skills.

We close Day One with live storytelling from people who transformed their love lives by treating love as a skill worth mastering, plus integration time so the work lands in your body, not just your notes.

There’ll be an hour lunch in the middle of our day together.

Day Two · Sunday · 10 AM – 4 PM

“Love has come to rule and transform; stay awake, my heart, stay awake.” — Rumi

Morning: Pillar 2, Radical Ownership

The morning integrates what shifted overnight, then moves into Neverland, a guided session for loosening old identities and reopening possibility in love. Through reflection and mind-mapping, you’ll map your patterns, desires, and leverage points, and take ownership of the ones you’ve been blaming on partners, exes, or the apps.

Then the event highlight: Hot Seat Coaching with master coaches Annie Lalla and Jordan Bowditch, where outdated relationship patterns get named, claimed, and interrupted live.

Closing: Pillar 4, Love Is a Practice

The weekend ends where the practice begins. Final frameworks turn everything you’ve learned into a practice you take home: acceptance, presence, forgiveness, and the question “What would love do here?” We close in circle, and you leave with tools you’ll keep using long after the weekend.
