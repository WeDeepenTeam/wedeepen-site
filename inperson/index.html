What would it be like if your love life was even better?

You’re optimizing your sleep, your bloodwork, your nervous system. And yet love is still the place that humbles you.

If you’re partnered: Maybe you’re more drained by the person you love than by anyone else, having the same fight you had two years ago, or quietly asking whether to stay or go. Or maybe you’re in a new relationship and you want to get it right this time.

If you’re solo: Maybe you’re tired of dating apps and events that go nowhere, unable to find someone as evolved as you are, or quietly wondering if the person you’re looking for even exists. Or maybe you’re moving through heartbreak and just want to feel like yourself again.

Whatever’s true for you, the current paradigm isn’t working. Relationships are going through a major redesign. People are unsatisfied in love; people don’t know how to make relationships work.

And, believe it or not, this is a good thing. Because when systems break down, that’s when they change. The breakdown is pushing us toward something better: conscious love. And that’s what this invitation is all about.

Beyond biohacking, love is the next frontier.

So what exactly is a conscious loving relationship?

It’s a relationship that has a purpose, and that purpose is growth.

Individual growth. Growth as a couple. Growth that makes the world a better place.

As of now, most people get into relationships to satisfy their own personal needs. This might work for a few years, but eventually the relationship fails us, and we end up unsatisfied as a result.

But when you come together with the intention of growth, your relationship strives toward something much greater than gratification. Your partnership becomes a journey of evolution, and you have an opportunity to expand more than you could alone. Deep satisfaction and long-term fulfillment arise as a result.

So if you’re someone who feels called to take love to the next level, WeDeepen was made for you.

WeDeepen In-Person

Spend two days getting even better at love.

Here’s what we’ll do. Over two days, you’ll learn and practice the 4 Pillars of a Conscious Relationship:

  1. No Attachment to Outcome. Growth comes first.
  2. Radical Ownership. Each person owns their own sh*t.
  3. All Feelings Are Welcome. No internal process is condemned.
  4. Love Is a Practice. Partnership, dating, and simply being human here on Earth: they’re all places to practice love.

With gratitude to Shelly Bullard for bringing the 4 Pillars of a Conscious Relationship to life for us to practice and pursue.

Day One · Saturday · 10 AM – 5 PM

Pillar 1, No Attachment to Outcome

We start with nervous-system grounding, because loving well comes easier when you feel calm and rooted in your body.

Christina teaches the first pillar: being more committed to growth than to making any relationship “work.” You’ll see why relationships built to preserve an outcome go stale, and what shifts when growth becomes the point, whether you’re dating, partnered, or starting over. She’ll share her most trusted relationship frames and give you language for patterns that have felt confusing or repetitive.

You’ll also zoom in on the Love Industry: the vastness that’s available that most people don’t know about and why love deserves the same intentional strategy you give your health and performance.

Pillar 2, Radical Ownership

Bonding closely with another person triggers old wounds; expect to feel abandoned, trapped, rejected, or overlooked at times. Those feelings stem from your own patterning, not from your partners. Dysfunctional patterns dissolve only when you take responsibility for them first. Relationships are a free will experience.

Neverland, a fun game we’ll play for loosening old identities and reopening possibility in love, leads into reflection and mind-mapping. You’ll map your patterns, desires, and leverage points, and take ownership of the ones you’ve been blaming on partners, exes, or the apps.

We close Day One with live storytelling from people who owned their patterns and transformed their love lives, plus the day’s favorite frames so you can remember what resonated most with you.

Special Guests include:

  • Melissa D. of BodyJoy Intimacy School on navigating erotic community spaces with confidence, including how a third can support a couple in keeping the spark alive.
  • Rachel Cuthbert on psychedelics and intimacy: which medicines support heart-opening, how to find a qualified facilitator, and what makes a safe, well-held experience.

There’ll be an hour lunch in the middle of our day together.

Day Two · Sunday · 10 AM – 4 PM

Pillar 3, All Feelings Are Welcome

The morning integrates what shifted overnight, then moves into radical honesty: making room to feel everything, and to express it without condemnation. It’s edgy territory, and it’s also where feeling known, seen, and truly understood begins.

You’ll learn more about what’s available to you through WeDeepen, and receive a presentation to go deeper and keep practicing love.

Pillar 4, Love Is a Practice

Event Highlight

Hot Seat Coaching

with master coaches Annie Lalla and Jordan Bowditch

Most of Sunday belongs to the hot seat. Person after person, you’ll watch “What would love do here?” answered live as outdated relationship patterns get named, claimed, and interrupted.

The weekend ends with you taking home greater acceptance, presence, forgiveness, and stretching your heart into vulnerable territories. We close in circle, and you leave with new resources for life, and a community to practice with.
