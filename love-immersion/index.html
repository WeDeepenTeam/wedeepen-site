What happens in three days

Every Love Immersion runs on the same four pillars: growth comes first with no attachment to outcome, you own your own experience, all feelings are welcome and no internal process is condemned, and the relationship is a place to practice love.

Around that, the weekend changes. Different faculty, different practices, a different room. There is live coaching where people work on the thing they actually came with, connection exercises that land in the body rather than the head, and long stretches of unstructured time where most of the real conversations happen.

You do not need a partner, a breakup, or a crisis to come. You need to be curious about how much better love can get.

Love reaches places that self-awareness alone may never touch. You can understand your history, recognize your patterns, and feel grounded on your own. Then closeness brings something older to the surface, like the fear of being rejected, abandoned, controlled, or not fully chosen.

Love Immersion lets you meet those moments as they happen, with skilled guidance and other people reflecting what is difficult to see from inside your own experience. The deeper work is not learning more about yourself. It is learning how to remain yourself when love asks the most of you.

The weekend begins with shared meals, play, conversation, and smaller experiences that allow trust to develop naturally. You still decide what you bring to your hot seat and how deeply you want to go. The experience is also guided by clear agreements around confidentiality, respect, and no unsolicited coaching.

Many people arrive a bit guarded. Watching others go first gives them permission to open in their own time.
