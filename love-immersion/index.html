What happens in three days

Every Love Immersion runs on the same four pillars: growth comes first with no attachment to outcome, you own your own experience, all feelings are welcome and no internal process is condemned, and the relationship is a place to practice love.

Around that, the weekend changes. Different faculty, different practices, a different room. There is live coaching where people work on the thing they actually came with, connection exercises that land in the body rather than the head, and long stretches of unstructured time where most of the real conversations happen.

You do not need a partner, a breakup, or a crisis to come. You need to be curious about how much better love can get.
