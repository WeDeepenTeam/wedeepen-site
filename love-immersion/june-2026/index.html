Love Immersion VII — Mastermind Your Love Life

VII

The best relationships don’t just happen — they’re built. Three days focused on your heart’s desires.

Reserve Your Spot

The Experience

Three days to build what chemistry alone never will.

Most of us were never taught how to love.

We learned how to build careers, manage money, and pursue success, but almost none of us were taught how to create healthy, thriving relationships.

So we rely on chemistry and hope for the best.

But chemistry isn’t what sustains love.

The strongest relationships are built through self-awareness, communication, emotional intelligence, and the willingness to grow.

Love Immersion is a three-day transformational experience for people who want to approach love with intention instead of leaving it to chance. Through expert coaching, workshops, play, plant medicine ceremony, and WeDeepen’s Four Pillars of a Conscious Relationship, you’ll develop the skills and capacity to create relationships that can grow in all directions.

Because love isn’t just something that happens to us.

It’s a trainable skill to be mastered.

The Arc

Three stages of learning to love.

Every Immersion moves through the same progression — the way a guarded heart actually opens once it trusts the ground it’s standing on.

Stage 1 · Remembering

Innocence

Before first heartbreak, you loved without bracing for impact. We start there — getting honest, into play, dropping the performance, seeing yourself clearly again. The ground steadies. You remember you are love.

Stage 2 · Releasing

De-Armoring

The patterns that once kept you safe are the same ones blowing up your relationships now. Through teaching, live coaching, and ceremony night, you meet them directly and let what’s no longer yours to carry fall away. This is where your true nature shines.

Stage 3 · Becoming

Ascension

You don’t leave with a rush of insight that fades by Monday. You leave with embodied capacity — new skills, greater range, and a clear read on the man or woman you’re becoming in love. This is the version of you “the work” was always pointing toward.

The Flow

What a Day Looks Like

Day 1 · Innocence

Arrive & Begin

Welcome circle, introductions, and intentions. Meet epic people who care about growth in love as much as you do. Through workshops, connection exercises, hot seat coaching, and a few surprises, you’ll begin building the trust and safety that make meaningful transformation possible.

Day 2 · De-Armoring

Get to the Root

Go deeper into the teachings, hot seat coaching, and continue opening to yourself and others. This is where long-held patterns come into view, old stories loosen their grip, and breakthroughs begin to emerge. Ceremony in the evening.

Day 3 · Ascension

Bring It Home

Integrate what you’ve learned through practical tools, clear commitments, and a closing ritual. You leave not with borrowed inspiration, but with greater self-awareness, a bigger heart, and an expanded capacity to love.

Throughout the weekend, you’ll share meals, meaningful conversations, and unforgettable moments with a community of people committed to growth, connection, and love.

Is This You?

Who Love Immersion is For

People with full, accomplished lives and one frontier left to take on: mastering love.

You’ve never lacked chemistry. You’ve lacked the skill to make it last.

The connection has always been there. So has the chaos. You’re done leaving your love life to whatever ignites and whatever explodes, and invested in creating something that actually holds.

Couples ready to go deeper

Your partnership is good. You want to deepen it. You’re here to rebuild, refine, and keep choosing each other everyday.

High-performers whose love life has been on autopilot

The career is handled. The body’s optimized. Your love life is the one arena you haven’t given your full attention, and you can feel it’s ready for it.

Anyone stepping into a new chapter

A new season, a clearer sense of what you want, a readiness to love with your eyes open. You’re not starting over. You’re starting truer.

The Company You Keep

Surround yourself with people who champion your evolution in love.

You rise to the level of the people around you. Spend three days among people committed to loving well, guided by Christina Weber and world-class Love Strategists, and the bar for what you’ll accept in your own love life rises and stays there.

The coaching is high-level and personal. The people beside you are doing brave, honest work in plain sight. And the learning doesn’t stop at your own breakthrough. Much of it comes from witnessing everyone else’s.

You leave with more than insights. You leave with allies, friendships, and a network that supports your growth long after the weekend ends.
