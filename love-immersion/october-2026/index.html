Count Me In

The Experience

Three days to meet the parts of you that emerge when love gets real.

Most of us reach adulthood with years of education and almost none in love. We inherit a few examples, follow chemistry, learn through heartbreak, and hope experience eventually becomes wisdom.

The Love Immersion Mastermind makes that learning intentional.

At the center are the Masterminding Love hot seats. You bring what is most alive in your love life to two expert coaches, who help you see beneath the story and into the pattern shaping it.

Then you learn again by witnessing everyone else take their turn. Their questions, defenses, breakthroughs, and courage have a way of illuminating your own.

Around the hot seats, the weekend unfolds through teachings, embodied practice, meaningful play, shared meals, a guided beautiful plant ceremony into expanded awareness, and deeply honest conversations. You leave with more clarity, practical ways to communicate and repair, and people who genuinely care what happens next.

Love can keep some of its mystery, but you don’t have to remain a mystery to yourself.

The Arc

Three stages of learning to love.

A guarded heart doesn’t open because the mind wants it to. It opens when it feels safe enough to do so.

Love Immersion guides you from Innocent, when you trusted love without question, through Armored, when hurt taught you to protect your heart, and into Skilled, where you reclaim vulnerability, apply wisdom, and deepen self-awareness.

You move through one stage per day.

Day 1 · Saturday · Remembering

Innocence

The Immersion begins at 9am. Come wearing a favorite accessory or piece of clothing. Settle into the mountaintop estate, share a welcome breakfast and gather for an opening circle of introductions, intentions, agreements and novel surprises.

You’ll laugh, play, get a feel for the people around you, and let the mystery of our newfound togetherness come naturally before the deeper work begins.

Today you Mastermind Love. Each individual or couple receives a focused 30-45 minute hot-seat session, while the rest of the group listens for the fears, needs, and protective strategies that echo through their own relationships.

Connecting with innocence here means recovering the willingness to meet another person openly, with everything life has taught you still intact.

What Saturday unlocks:

  • ✓A clearer awareness of how quickly past experiences shape new connections
  • ✓More freedom to be present without performing, impressing, or overthinking
  • ✓Renewed access to curiosity, play, and the openness that makes intimacy so joyful

Day 2 · Sunday · Releasing

De-Armoring

You continue masterminding love. It’ll seem less abstract. You see how control can disguise fear, how independence can become distance, and how the desire to be chosen can lead someone away from what they truly want. What is difficult to recognize in yourself often becomes unmistakable when witnessed in another.

You’ll also find plenty of laughter, play, and unexpected moments that remind you how good it feels to be fully alive. Between the insight and the fun, people often find themselves seeing love and their own lives from an elevated perspective.

The evening moves into a guided beautiful plant ceremony, giving the two days of insights somewhere deeper to land. It’s an amazing and inspiring environment for softening around what you’ve been protecting, feeling what matters more clearly, and leaving with a stronger sense of what you want to carry into your relationships.

What Sunday unlocks:

  • ✓Greater awareness of the hidden defenses influencing who you choose and how you connect
  • ✓A higher capacity to remain open and choose consciously when old fears are activated
  • ✓Practical tools for communicating clearly, repairing conflict, and asking for what you need

Day 3 · Monday · Becoming

Skilled Love

Monday starts slower with a late breakfast. You’ll return to teachings, embodied practice, reflection, and a closing ceremony. By now, the patterns are easier to see, and the focus becomes what you want to do differently when they appear again.

A light and fun graduation ceremony puts a bow on the whole experience as you shape the conversations, requests, boundaries, repairs, and choices that will carry more love and grace into your relationships.

You’ll write a love letter to someone who matters to you, which we’ll mail after the Immersion, receive your graduation gift, revisit the moments that mattered, and leave around 2:00 PM.

What Monday unlocks:

  • ✓Greater confidence making relationship decisions from clarity rather than fear or habit
  • ✓Time to reflect, name what changed, and decide how to bring that into your daily life
  • ✓People who will remember what opened in you and help you keep choosing it

Who Love Immersion Is For?

Anyone who understands that something as important as love deserves more than instinct, chemistry, and good intentions.

Some come to deepen a beautiful partnership. Some are standing at a crossroads. Some come with a general sense that they are failing at love. Others arrive on their own because they know the next chapter has to begin with them.

High achievers ready to give love their full attention

You know how to seek expertise, do the work, and grow. Love is the one place you have mostly relied on instinct and lessons learned the hard way. You are ready to meet it with the same intention you bring to everything else.

Couples ready to go deeper

Your relationship may be anywhere on the spectrum from thriving to strained, but you want to understand each other more clearly, repair without keeping score, or nurture the connection into something even deeper, freer, and more alive.

Anyone stepping into a new chapter

You may be healing from heartbreak, deciding whether to stay or leave, or preparing to love differently next time. You can arrive without a partner and make this chapter entirely your own. Many have.

The relationships you build here are the other half of the curriculum.

You may arrive among strangers or familiar faces, but never random ones. Everyone has chosen to invest in becoming better at love. With the pressure to impress or pursue removed, Love Immersion becomes a place where people can relax and be themselves.

Individuals and couples learn side by side. You watch people name what they want, own what they’ve avoided, and stay connected through interactions usually hidden behind closed doors. Again and again, someone else’s honesty makes something in your own life easier to see.

By the time you leave, these aren’t just people who attended the same retreat. You’ve laughed with them, maybe cried with them, and moved past barriers with them. They have seen what you carried in, what opened up, and how you want to live from here on. Many become trusted friends, mentors, collaborators, matchmakers, and lifelong allies as your love story continues to unfold.
