The Experience

Three days to build what chemistry alone never will.

You’ve learned how to succeed in business, create wealth, and pursue ambitious goals. But few of us were ever taught how to create an exceptional relationship.

Most people leave love to chance. The men and women who create extraordinary partnerships don’t.

Through world-class coaching, immersive workshops, play, and meaningful connection, you’ll build what actually holds a relationship together: knowing yourself, saying the true thing, and repairing well when it breaks.

Because love isn’t luck.

It’s a trainable skill we master together.

The Arc

Three stages of learning to love.

A guarded heart doesn’t open because the mind wants it to. It opens when it feels safe enough to do so.

Love Immersion guides you from Innocent (when you trusted love without question), through Armored (when hurt taught you to protect your heart), and into Skilled (when you reclaim vulnerability, wisdom, and deeper self-awareness) — one stage per day, with a final morning to rest and recover.

Day 1 · Friday Evening · Remembering

Innocence

The immersion opens at 5pm. Settle into the beauty of the venue, share a welcome dinner, and gather for our opening circle — setting intentions, sharing love codes, and beginning the deepening together, with a few surprises. Before first heartbreak, you loved without bracing for impact. We start there. You remember you are love.

Day 2 · Saturday · Releasing

De-Armoring

A full day of masterminding. You take the hot seat for thirty to forty-five minutes with two master coaches, on whatever is most alive in your love life. Everyone else witnesses, then takes their turn. The patterns that once kept you safe are often the same ones disrupting your relationships — here you meet them directly, and long-held stories loosen their grip. At 6pm we enter the medicine ceremony, held in a safe, sacred container that carries us into the night.

Day 3 · Sunday · Becoming

Skilled Love

Integration day. We ease in with brunch, then give space for reflection through shared learning, thought-provoking activities, and a final round of hot seat coaching. You don’t leave with a rush of insight that fades by Monday — you leave with embodied capacity and a clear sense of the man or woman you’re becoming in love. We complete with our closing ceremony at 4pm, then stay for dinner together and an evening of playful mischief.

Monday Morning · Spaciousness

Rest and Recover

Breakfast at 9am, then the sauna, pool, cold plunge, and hot tub until we say goodbye at noon. We built the last morning soft on purpose, so you land instead of getting flung straight back into your life.

Throughout the immersion, you’ll share meals, meaningful conversations, and unforgettable moments with a community of people committed to growth, connection, and love.

Is This You?

Who Love Immersion is For

People with full, accomplished lives and one frontier left to take on: mastering love.

The connection has always been there. So has the chaos. You’re done leaving your love life to whatever ignites and whatever explodes, and invested in creating something that actually holds.

Couples ready to go deeper

Your partnership is good. You want to deepen it. You’re here to rebuild, refine, and keep choosing each other everyday.

High-performers whose love life has been on autopilot

Anyone stepping into a new chapter

The Company You Keep

The people beside you are the other half of the curriculum.

You rise to the level of the people around you. Spend three days among people committed to loving well, and the bar for what you’ll accept in your own love life rises and stays there.

The coaching is high-level and personal. The people beside you are doing brave, honest work in plain sight. And the learning doesn’t stop at your own breakthrough. Much of it comes from witnessing everyone else’s.

You leave with more than insights. You leave with allies, friendships, and a network that supports your growth long after the weekend ends.
