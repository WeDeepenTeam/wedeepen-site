What we practice together

  • Choosing someone who fits the life you actually want, not just the chemistry.
  • Catching your old pattern while it’s happening, instead of three months in.
  • Staying yourself in a relationship. No more shrinking.
  • Saying what you need sooner, with kindness, without the speech you rehearsed for a week.
  • Telling the difference between someone choosing you and someone keeping you around.
  • Staying close through a hard conversation, and repairing before hurt turns into distance.
  • Keeping desire alive: missing each other and reuniting, seeing your partner through new eyes, accomplishing something together.
