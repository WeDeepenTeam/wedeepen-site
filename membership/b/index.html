Core framework

The Four Pillars of a Conscious Relationship

The old model said: find the right person, meet each other’s needs, live happily ever after. Maybe it works. Most likely, it doesn’t. A conscious relationship has a different purpose: growth. Partnered, dating, or solo, this is how we practice.

1

No Attachment to Outcome. Growth Comes First. (Prioritize growth)

No more shrinking to keep the peace. Growth keeps love alive. Ask “Who do I get to become here?” instead of “How do I make this work?”

2

Both Partners Agree to Own Their Own Sh*t.

Closeness will find your old wounds. When something stings, pause and ask: what part of this is mine? Blame keeps you stuck. Ownership sets you free.

3

All Feelings Are Welcome. No Internal Process Is Condemned.

There’s room to feel everything: jealousy, desire, grief, anger, insecurity, joy. And room to share it. You’d rather know each other than keep each other comfortable.

4

The Relationship Is a Place to Practice Love.

Love shows up in your hello, your patience on a hard day, and staying open when it’s easier to shut down. Keep asking: what would love do here?

Adapted from the work of therapist Shelly Bullard, MFT.
