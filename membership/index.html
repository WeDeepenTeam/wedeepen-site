The skill of love

Love has a much messier learning curve than most things we get good at.

We develop skills in work, health, sports, and nearly every other part of life through learning, practice, and feedback. Love is usually left to experience.

WeDeepen gives you a place to build the skill of love more deliberately, including learning to tell the difference between:

Chemistry

vs.

compatibility

A rough patch

vs.

a repeating pattern

Healthy compromise

vs.

giving too much away

A real concern

vs.

an old fear getting triggered
