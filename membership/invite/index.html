Love Immersion VIII · October 2026

Our eighth Love Immersion was epic.

This October in Austin we hosted our eighth Love Immersion: three days with our faculty and a room full of people practicing love out loud. It was an incredible weekend. And honestly, it gets better and better each time.

Here’s what I keep seeing. Three days can open something huge. Then everyone goes home, and love happens on an ordinary weeknight. In the text you don’t know how to answer. In the conversation you keep putting off.

So we built a room you can come back to every week.

A practice room, not a library. Reps, feedback, and people who notice. Love is a trainable skill, and skills need reps.
