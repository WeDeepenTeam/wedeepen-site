A guarded heart doesn’t open just because the mind wants it to. It opens when it feels safe enough. So we start by building trust, then turn up the wildly fun, I-didn’t-know-sober-could-feel-this-good play.

The Agreements

Freedom feels better with care.

The magic of a WeDeepen gathering comes from how you show up for yourself and one another.

These shared agreements are part of what makes this celebration so special. Imagine being surrounded by people who value friendship, practice love, own their sh*t, and know how to have a ridiculously good time.

Here’s what you’re saying yes to.

  1. 1

    High on life. 100% substance-free.

    You’re bringing your own natural chemistry! No alcohol, cannabis, psychedelics, or recreational substances from arrival on Dec 30 through departure on Jan 1.

  2. 2

    Come ready to celebrate.

    Fasting, ceremonies, plant medicine, cleansing, or deep healing work are best completed before you arrive, with plenty of time to integrate. Then come enjoy the fruits of your inner work and have some serious fun!

  3. 3

    Friends first. Love always wins.

    The best connections begin with genuine curiosity. Get to know each other. Flirt if the sparks fly. Celebrate couples in love, honor their partnerships, and cheer each other on. Be the kind of friend you’d love to have.

  4. 4

    Be here for all of it.

    From opening circle to closing circle, give yourself the gift of being fully present. Put your phone away during sessions. The memories are yours to make.

  5. 5

    Keep the magic sacred.

    What’s shared personally stays private. Ask before photographing or posting anyone, and respect their answer. Some moments are even sweeter when they’re just yours.

  6. 6

    Own your sh*t. Love yourself through it.

    You’re the CEO of your emotional landscape. Honor your needs, speak your truth with kindness, and take responsibility for your experience. All feelings are welcome, and you’re responsible for how you express them.

  7. 7

    Bring your sense of humor. And your heart.

    Life is one big rom-com, and you’re the star. Laugh at yourself, give others the benefit of the doubt, and celebrate each other’s wins. Bring the kind of energy you’d love to be surrounded by.

  8. 8

    Treat the mansion like it’s yours. In the best way.

    Enjoy this beautiful home and care for it as you would your own. Respect the property, follow the house rules, and be ready to depart by 10 AM on Jan 1.

The magic is in what you create together.
