The Night

Long-term fulfillment over instant gratification.

Most New Year’s Eves are built for instant gratification: a drink, a kiss, a blur, and a January that looks exactly like December.

This one is built for what lasts.

Starting the evening of December 30, 30 of us gather at the Gatsby Mansion clear-headed and fully awake to choose the 2027 we’re stepping into. We’ll settle our nervous systems, drop what’s been draining us, get honest about what we truly desire, and build a picture of the year so vivid our bodies start to believe it.

Then at midnight we don’t make wishes. We make a choice. Together.

Structure · Play · Fun · Relationship

How the night flows

  1. 1

    Arrive and land

    Breathwork and settling the body. What does your nervous system need for this to be an epic New Year’s?

  2. 2

    Cut the cords

    Name what 2026 took from you: the habits, people, and patterns that fed on your energy. Then let them go.

  3. 3

    Your goal, your door

    Get clear on what you truly desire, not what you think you should want.

  4. 4

    Build your slide

    See your 2027 in the present tense and feel it in your body, alone and then with a partner.

  5. 5

    Play

    Dancing, games, Truth or Dare, and the kind of lightness that cracks a room wide open.

  6. 6

    Midnight

    No wishes. A calm, shared decision as we step onto the 2027 line together.

  7. 7

    The mirror

    Leave connected to the people who will hold your 2027 with you.
