Opening Night to New Year’s Morning

Long-term fulfillment over instant gratification.

Most New Year’s Eves are built for instant gratification: a drink, a kiss, a blur, and a January that looks exactly like December.

This one is built for what lasts.

From the evening of December 30 to the morning of New Year’s Day, 30 of us gather at the Gatsby Mansion clear-headed and fully awake to choose the 2027 we’re stepping into. We’ll settle our nervous systems, drop what’s been draining us, get honest about what we truly desire, and build a picture of the year so vivid our bodies start to believe it.

Then at midnight we don’t make wishes. We make a choice. Together.
