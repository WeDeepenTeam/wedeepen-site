Jay was supposed to be in Colorado by now.

He’s spent most of his life on the move, often walking in as the stranger. He came to Love Immersion VIII planning to leave right after.

A week later, on our integration call, he told us he was still in Austin. And he was staying.

He said it was a weekend he never wanted to end. He’d finally found people who felt like home.

I’ve been thinking about Jay as this year closes. The holidays have a way of showing us exactly where we belong, and what we’re still seeking.

Here’s a small practice for today.

Pick up a pen and finish this sentence: “In 2027, I want to feel...”

Choose a feeling rather than a thing. Then share it with a friend who’d gossip about you in the best way.

Once you tell somebody, it gets a lot harder to quietly let it slide.

Now imagine saying exactly what you desire to a group of incredible people who are all rooting for you.

That’s what we’re doing December 30 to January 1 at the Gatsby Mansion in Austin, Texas.

Every variation of what you want already exists.

You get to choose it, specifically.

That’s the heart of Reality Transurfing, and Yossef Sagi will lead us there.

Together we’ll map out the 2027 you’re choosing, down to how it feels on an ordinary Tuesday, release what’s keeping you on the old track, and slide into the reality you really want.

He weaves it together with Tantra, Tibetan meditation, breath and energy work, and a little eros magic, so your 2027 lands in your body as well as your mind.

And then we celebrate. On New Year’s Eve, in velvet or silk, there’s a feast, Truth or Dare, a little flirtation, and lots of dancing. When midnight comes, everyone around you is fully present, and so are you. 100% substance-free.

I booked a mansion for us, and I’m inviting you, along with the kind of people Jay found. People who take their health and longevity seriously, and who know the quality of their life comes down to the quality of their relationships.

Here’s what it looks like when we get together:

The Gatsby sits in the hills outside Austin, and for two days it’s ours.

On January 1, you walk out into the first sunrise of a year you’ve already chosen, with new friends who heard you claim it and won’t let you forget it.

Melissa D. of BodyJoy Intimacy School and I are guiding alongside Yossef. Come solo, as a couple, or with a friend. Intimate, 30 people tops.

Your ticket includes four shared meals, every guided experience, and the whole mansion for the celebration.
