Support

Need a hand?

Locked out of the community, missing a Zoom link, or something just isn't working? Start here. A real person on the WeDeepen team reads every message.

Can't log in?

  1. Go to circle.wedeepen.com and enter the email you joined with.
  2. Request a login code. Check spam and promotions if it doesn't show up within a few minutes.
  3. Not sure which email you used? Send us the ones you think it could be, using the form below.

Missing a Zoom link?

RSVP to the session in the Events Calendar. The link appears on the event page and in your reminder email.

Contact support

Still stuck? Tell us what's going on and we'll get back to you by email.

Prefer email? Write to team@wedeepen.com.

If you're in crisis

Call or text 988. In immediate danger, call 911. For confidential domestic violence support, call 1-800-799-7233 or text START to 88788.
